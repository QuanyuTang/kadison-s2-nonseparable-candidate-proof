# Unverified Candidate Proof for the Nonseparable Case of Kadison's Problem S.2

> **Status: independently unverified candidate proof. No claim is made that the main theorem has been proved.**

In 1967, Richard V. Kadison asked whether every type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor has an orthonormal basis, with respect to its trace, consisting of unitaries.

The problem is listed as **Problem S.2 (Kadison 1967)** on Jesse Peterson's webpage *Open problems in operator algebras*. The separable case has been settled affirmatively, while the nonseparable case is still listed there as open.

This page presents an independently unverified AI-generated candidate proof for the remaining nonseparable case. The manuscript was generated by OpenAI's GPT-5.5 Pro through a prompting, compilation, and curation process coordinated by Yixin He and Quanyu Tang.
